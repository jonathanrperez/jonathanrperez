Research interests

Research Professor in Theoretical Physics specializing in quantum field theory, high-energy physics, and modern methods for calculating scattering amplitudes. My work focuses on studying the mathematical structures underlying quantum theories, including helicity methods, Weyl spinors, supersymmetry, and advanced perturbative techniques.

I have experience developing analytical and symbolic calculations using computational tools such as Mathematica and specialized packages for quantum field theory. My research interests include scattering amplitudes, algebraic methods applied to QFT, supergravity, quantum chromodynamics, and new mathematical tools for understanding fundamental interactions.

In addition to scientific research, I have experience in university teaching, student mentoring, and communicating scientific results through publications, seminars, and academic conferences.
