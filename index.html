About me

Theoretical Physicist and Research Professor specializing in Quantum Field Theory, High Energy Physics, and modern scattering amplitude methods. My research focuses on the mathematical structures of quantum theories, including spinor-helicity techniques, supersymmetry, supergravity, and perturbative approaches to fundamental interactions. Experienced in symbolic computation with Mathematica and advanced computational tools for quantum field theory. Passionate about research, university teaching, and mentoring young scientists.
